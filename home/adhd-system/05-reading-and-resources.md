# Reading & Resources

## Read / in progress
- **ADHD 2.0** — Hallowell & Ratey. In progress (print; not on Spotify audio as of March 2026).
- **Scattered Minds** — Gabor Maté. Read. Justin's take: the maternal-cause framing is a problem, and it underplays the biological basis of attention differences. Don't recommend it uncritically.
- **Unreasonable Hospitality** — Will Guidara. Foundational for the Margie's team (not ADHD, but shapes how he thinks).

## On the shelf, not yet read
- **The ADHD Entrepreneur's Guide** — owned, unread.

## Recommended, not yet read
- **Taking Charge of Adult ADHD** — Russell Barkley. Evidence-based; strong on executive function, time management, follow-through. Best fit for "research-grounded."
- **Driven to Distraction** — Hallowell & Ratey. The classic; reframes ADHD as difference, not deficit.
- **How to Keep House While Drowning** — KC Davis. Short, compassionate, about functional systems when executive function is unreliable. Good fit for the home-organization priority.
- **Order from Chaos** — Jaclyn Paul. Systems that work with an ADHD brain.
- **Smart but Scattered** — Dawson & Guare. Breaks executive function into specific skills, with self-assessment questionnaires to find the weakest ones.
- **The ADHD Effect on Marriage** — Melissa Orlov. Relevant to the priority of rebuilding trust with Becky and to the "Becky as working memory" problem.
- **ADHD is Awesome** — Penn & Kim Holderness. Lighter, relatable.

## Self-assessment tools mentioned
- Smart but Scattered executive-skills questionnaire (11 skills).
- Brown Executive Function/Attention Scales (activation, focus, effort, emotion, memory, action) — normally given by a clinician.

## For Mason
**Smart but Scattered Teens** (Dawson & Guare) — frames it as "how your brain works best," not "what's wrong with you."

## How Justin listens
Audiobooks only work during hands-on, manual-labor projects (shop work, repairs, facility blocks). They don't work during computer work, and sitting still to listen leads to falling asleep. Default to print for ADHD reading; save audio for build days. Spotify for convenience; Libby through Anoka County Library.

## On coaching programs
Skeptical of marketing-heavy ADHD courses (vague promises, premium tiers, upsells). Good support builds custom systems and accountability in the real context. Therapy already covers much of what a generic course would sell.
