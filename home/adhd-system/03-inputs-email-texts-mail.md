# Processing Inputs — Email, Texts, Mail

*Final email system from Oct 1, 2026; text protocol from July; physical-mail rules from Feb.*

---

## Email

**Core rule:** The inbox is a loading dock, not a warehouse. Tasks live in Todoist and only in Todoist. Two lists means checking both and trusting neither.

**Every email has three fates:**
1. **Reply now** — under two minutes. Do it, archive.
2. **Push to Todoist, then archive** — anything that needs action.
3. **Archive** — everything else.

No folders, no labels, no filing. Gmail search is enough.

**Capture mechanic:** Gmail → Todoist add-on. It links to the live thread, so the task opens into the real conversation, including later replies.
- **Archive, never delete.** The task is a pointer. Delete the thread and the task becomes a riddle.
- **Ignore the add-on's fields** — no project, date, priority, or description. Inbox, five plain words. Sorting happens at the planning meeting.
- **Fallback:** Todoist forwarding address saved as a contact named "Todoist." If the add-on isn't there (some mobile threads), forward and archive. Don't stall.
- Garbage subject? Retitle to five words ("order hood filter Grackle") and nothing more.

**Edge case:** If the action is just a thoughtful reply (Lou asking something real, a vendor negotiation), same rule — capture, archive, reply when the task comes up. *Scanning is not doing, even when the doing is email.*

**Implementation order:**
1. Email bankruptcy — archive everything older than the cutoff, unread, no triage.
2. Set up the add-on and the forwarding contact.
3. Run three fates on new mail only, inside the daily Scan.

Backlog and habit are two projects. Run them together and the backlog eats the habit.

**This is a rock.** It needs a calendar block and a done-line. **Done = bankruptcy declared and add-on working.** Not inbox zero on the backlog.

## Texts (bankruptcy protocol, 30 min)

1. **Mass-clear first.** Edit → Select all → Mark as Read. Badge to zero before reading anything. Ignore unknown/filtered senders.
2. **Work the thread list, not messages.** Only threads active in the last two weeks. Read the last screen only. Reply now (<2 min) / dictate a capture / move on. Group chats get a five-second skim.
3. **Older than two weeks: closed, unopened.** No "shame archaeology."
4. **The trap:** Don't open with an apology paragraph on an old text. One fresh line: "Hey, this got buried — still want to grab that time?" If you can't tell whether it's still live, it isn't.

## Physical mail and paper

- Paper with an action attached goes digital fast: photograph it, email/forward it where it needs to go, then toss the original.
- Checks: deposit at the next bank trip (no mobile deposit at current bank) — put the check where you'll grab it on the way out, not in a pile.
- Something sent and now waiting on a reply: Gmail **snooze** on the sent email works as a lightweight Waiting For. Not everything needs a Todoist entry.

## Other inboxes in the daily Scan
Voicemail, manager log notes, Airtable maintenance log — same three verdicts, same 24-hour "seen" standard.
